Services

Expertise across the full lifecycle of disaster recovery

From pre-disaster planning with expert facilitation, through program design, implementation, and closeout. I support state and local governments and their consulting partners, with a focus on CDBG-DR, FEMA IA, and integrating FEMA PA and HMA with CDBG-DR and other resources.

CDBG-DR Disaster Housing FEMA Programs Program Stand-Up Planning and Facilitation Capture Support
01

CDBG-DR

Action Plans built for rapid HUD approval, and programs designed to move funding out the door while controlling administrative and activity delivery costs.

Action Plan Development

  • Unmet needs assessments
  • Program selection and design
  • Method of distribution and implementation strategy
  • Citizen participation and stakeholder engagement
  • Financial and grants management certification
  • Action Plan amendments

Program Implementation Support

  • Operational planning and business process development
  • Policy and procedure development
  • Case management and eligibility review process design and implementation
  • Program management and oversight
  • Subrecipient management and technical assistance
  • HUD compliance, including Federal Register requirements, 2 CFR 200, duplication of benefits, and environmental review

Specialized Program Design

  • Housing recovery programs (homeowner, rental, multifamily)
  • Infrastructure programs integrating PA and HMGP
  • Economic revitalization
  • Mitigation and resilience programs
02

Disaster Housing

Housing strategy across the continuum, from sheltering to permanent housing, including creative use of existing ("blue sky") HUD funding and waivers before CDBG-DR arrives.

Housing Recovery Strategy and Planning

  • Housing recovery needs assessment
  • Multi-program integration (for example, TSA, IHP, VOAD, and CDBG-DR)
  • Strategies for bridging the gap from FEMA programs to CDBG-DR
  • Pre-disaster housing recovery planning with expert facilitation
  • Housing Recovery Support Function (RSF) planning and execution

Housing Program Implementation

  • Homeowner repair and reconstruction programs
  • Affordable multifamily housing development, including CDBG-DR paired with LIHTC
  • Innovative housing solutions and technology evaluation
  • Temporary housing strategies
  • Intake, eligibility review, and case management process design and implementation
03

FEMA Programs

An insider's view of Individual Assistance policy and operations from FEMA Headquarters, and match strategies that pair CDBG-DR with Public Assistance and Hazard Mitigation projects.

Individual Assistance (IA)

  • IA policy and program expertise
  • Direct housing mission planning and operations
  • Advisory support for requesting specific FEMA IA program activations to meet survivor needs
  • Integrating state, local, and VOAD operations with available FEMA support
  • Sheltering and mass care cost recovery through Public Assistance

Public Assistance (PA)

  • Infrastructure recovery planning
  • Integration with CDBG-DR programs
  • Policy and guidance interpretation

Hazard Mitigation Assistance (HMA)

  • Project layering with CDBG-DR and other resources
  • Mitigation project development and evaluation
  • Policy and guidance interpretation, from Unified Guidance to portfolio management
04

Program Stand-Up and Operations

Rapid stand-up of the people, processes, and controls that get assistance to applicants.

Operations

  • Rapid stand-up of applicant intake, case management, eligibility, and award operations
  • Recruiting, training, and quality control for large surge teams

Policies and Procedures

  • Policies, procedures, and manuals for CDBG-DR, Emergency Rental Assistance (ERAP), and CARES Act programs
05

Recovery Planning and Facilitation

Planning and facilitation that help state and federal leaders reach decisions on recovery operations.

Planning

  • Pre-disaster recovery planning, including Housing Recovery Support Function (RSF) annexes
  • Policy and guidance development, including the National Disaster Recovery Framework (NDRF) update

Facilitation

  • Executive facilitation that drives state and federal decisions on recovery operations
06

Capture and Proposal Support

Support for national firms pursuing FEMA and CDBG-DR work, from a small business teaming partner.

Capture

  • Capture strategy, client pre-positioning, and teaming for FEMA and CDBG-DR pursuits

Proposals

  • Technical approach, staffing plan, and pricing strategy review
  • Key personnel and oral presentation support
