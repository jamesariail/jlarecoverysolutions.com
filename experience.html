Experience

Two decades of disaster recovery leadership

20+ years supporting disaster recovery programs across housing, infrastructure, and mitigation. The engagements below were led by James Ariail in senior roles, including with prior firms.

Key Projects

Selected project experience

2024–2025

City of Asheville CDBG-DR Action Plan

City of Asheville, NC
Program Manager

Led development of the City's first CDBG-DR Action Plan for its $225M allocation following Hurricane Helene, the most destructive storm to hit the region in modern history.

  • Developed the Action Plan and received HUD approval within 90 days of the Allocation Announcement Notice, with no required changes
  • Worked with multiple City stakeholders, including the City Manager, Mayor, and City Council members, to design programs and develop a method of distribution that dovetailed with other funding sources to meet complex recovery requirements
  • Designed programs for housing, infrastructure, and economic recovery
  • Supported the City in augmenting its procurement and financial management policies and procedures to successfully receive financial and grants management certification from HUD
2020–2025

Nebraska CDBG-DR Program Management

Nebraska Department of Economic Development
Program Manager

Led Action Plan development and comprehensive grants management and implementation support for $108M in CDBG-DR funding following severe flooding events.

  • Developed the Action Plan and subsequent amendments
  • Led implementation over the lifecycle of the grant
  • Created an Infrastructure Match Program integrating PA and HMGP
  • Implemented housing programs, including a Low-Income Housing Tax Credit (LIHTC) "piggyback" program for affordable multifamily housing development
2021–2022

Louisiana ERAP Grants Administration

Louisiana Housing Corporation
Grants Administration Director

Led case management, eligibility review, and policy development for the statewide Emergency Rental Assistance Program (ERAP) during the COVID-19 pandemic.

  • Developed a comprehensive policy framework, including duplication of benefits (DOB) verification procedures
  • Led efforts to design and implement the staffing model, training, and business processes for intake, eligibility review, quality control, appeals, and compliance monitoring
