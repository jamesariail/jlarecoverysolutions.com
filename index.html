Why JLA Recovery Solutions

FEMA and HUD depth in one advisor

Deep expertise in both FEMA programs and CDBG-DR, and in how they fit together, is uncommon in this field. It helps clients layer funding sources, manage duplication of benefits, and design programs that move recovery dollars quickly and compliantly.

  • Federal insight. Individual Assistance leadership experience at FEMA Headquarters, and CDBG-DR program leadership since the Hurricane Ike recovery.
  • Proven HUD results. Asheville's first CDBG-DR Action Plan, developed in under 90 days and approved by HUD with no required changes.
  • Rapid mobilization. 100+ shelter staff deployed in 72 to 96 hours (Harvey); 200+ staff at peak production in three weeks (Build It Back).
  • Technology-enabled delivery. Developer of the DisasterData iOS app; data and AI tools speed routine work, with senior expert review of every work product.

Core Competencies

Expertise across the full recovery lifecycle

From pre-disaster planning through program design, stand-up, implementation, and closeout.

CDBG-DR Program Design and Implementation

  • Action Plans built for rapid HUD approval, with financial and grants management certifications
  • Program design that moves funding out the door while controlling administrative and activity delivery costs
  • Compliance with Federal Register requirements, 2 CFR 200, duplication of benefits, and environmental review

FEMA Programs (PA, IA, HMA)

  • Insider's view of IA policy and operations from FEMA Headquarters, including Direct Housing and Mass Care
  • Match strategies pairing CDBG-DR with PA and Hazard Mitigation Grant Program (HMGP) projects
  • HMA grant policy and implementation support, from Unified Guidance to portfolio management

Disaster Housing Recovery

  • Housing strategy across the continuum, from sheltering to permanent housing
  • Creative use of existing ("blue sky") HUD funding and waivers before CDBG-DR arrives
  • Multifamily housing pairing CDBG-DR with Low-Income Housing Tax Credits (LIHTC)
  • Alternative housing technology evaluation

Program Stand-Up and Operations

  • Rapid stand-up of applicant intake, case management, eligibility, and award operations
  • Recruiting, training, and quality control for large surge teams
  • Policies, procedures, and manuals for CDBG-DR, Emergency Rental Assistance (ERAP), and CARES Act programs

Recovery Planning and Facilitation

  • Pre-disaster recovery planning, including Housing Recovery Support Function (RSF) annexes
  • Executive facilitation that drives state and federal decisions on recovery operations
  • Policy and guidance development, including the National Disaster Recovery Framework (NDRF) update

Capture and Proposal Support

  • Capture strategy, client pre-positioning, and teaming for FEMA and CDBG-DR pursuits
  • Technical approach, staffing plan, and pricing strategy review
  • Key personnel and oral presentation support
