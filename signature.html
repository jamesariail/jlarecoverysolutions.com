Install Your Email Signature

One tap copies the whole signature—logo, text, and links—so you never have to select it by hand.

Now paste it into Mail

1Open Settings → Apps → Mail → Signature (older iOS: Settings → Mail → Signature).
2Tap the signature box, select any existing text, and delete it.
3Tap and hold in the empty box, then tap Paste.
4If the logo or colors look plain, shake your phone and tap Undo — it reverts to the formatted version.
Tip: send yourself a test email afterward to confirm the logo and links show up.
